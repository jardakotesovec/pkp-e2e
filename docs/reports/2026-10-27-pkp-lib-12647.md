edge of the window now opens above its button or shifts sideways, so

## Finding 1 — Tab inside an open "More Actions" menu does nothing

- **Severity** low
- **Effort** small
- **Kind** regression
- **Affects**
  - main: OJS (walked base and head), OMP, OPS (head walked)

### Impact

- **Lost**: leaving a menu with Tab. A keyboard user who opens a row's
  "…" and then decides to move on presses Tab, and nothing happens.
- **Who**: keyboard and screen-reader users, on every "More Actions"
  menu of the back office (Users & Roles, participants, reviewers,
  files, galleys, dashboard bulk actions and the rest).
- **Way round**: Escape closes the menu and puts the focus back on its
  button. So this is not a keyboard trap (WCAG 2.1.2 holds).

Low: the way out exists and is the usual one, but the change works
against the issue's own accessibility aim.

### Steps to reproduce

1. As a journal manager, open Settings › Users & Roles.
2. Press Tab until a row's "…" button has the focus, then press Enter.
   The menu opens with the focus on "Edit".
3. Press Tab.

Expected (before the PR): the menu closes and the focus moves on.
Actual: the menu stays open and the focus stays on "Edit". Shift+Tab
does the same.

### Cause

reka-ui's menu content swallows Tab: `Menu/MenuContentImpl.js` (reka-ui
2.8.0), `if (event.key === "Tab") event.preventDefault();` for a keydown
inside the content. headlessui's `MenuItems` closed the menu on Tab and
moved the focus to the element after (or before) the button.
`DropdownActions.vue` adds no Tab handling of its own.

### Proposed fix

The team's route (round 2): upgrade reka-ui to 2.10.1 or later,
`pkp/pkp-lib#13634`. The round-1 alternative inside the component,
not tried: close the menu on Tab in `DropdownActions.vue`, holding the
open state:

```vue
<DropdownMenuRoot v-model:open="isOpen" :modal="false" :dir="dir">
	…
	<DropdownMenuContent … @keydown.tab="isOpen = false">
```

with `const isOpen = ref(false);`. reka-ui then returns the focus to the
button, so the next Tab moves on from there. To land one step further,
as headlessui did, handle `@close-auto-focus` and focus the next
tabbable element.

## Finding 2 — a disabled link action is followed when clicked (closed in round 2)

Closed. It is fixed at the head `f7a5fa9174` and was not a regression.
Round 2 walked the base (`1f9fb95e53`, headlessui) on OJS. There, a click
on the disabled link goes to its address as well (`defaultPrevented`
false, no action handler run). headlessui 1.7.23's `mergeProps`
(`utils/render.js`) drops every event handler from a disabled item,
including its own `preventDefault()`, so nothing stops the browser.
Round 1 took the base from reading `MenuItem`'s click handler and got
this wrong. At `f7a5fa9174` the disabled link renders without an
`href` and with `data-disabled`, greyed (`text-disabled`). A click goes
nowhere, the menu stays open and no handler runs, and the arrow keys
skip the item. An enabled link action beside it still navigates and
runs its handler. Walked on OJS, OMP and OPS. The round-1 text
follows for the record.


- **Severity** low
- **Effort** small
- **Kind** regression
- **Affects**
  - main: OJS (head walked with an action a plugin would add; base from code), OMP, OPS (code)

### Impact

- **Lost**: the guarantee that a disabled action does nothing.
- **Who**: users of a plugin, or of a future screen, that offers a link
  action (`url`) marked `disabled`. No such action ships in OJS, OMP or
  OPS today: every disabled action there is a button action, and it
  stays unselectable.
- **Way round**: none needed today.

Low: nothing in the apps reaches it.

### Steps to reproduce

1. Add a "More Actions" item that is a link and disabled. For example,
   on Users & Roles, `pkp.registry.storeExtendFn('userAccessManager',
   'getItemActions', …)` appending `{label: 'Disabled link', url:
   '<any address>', disabled: true}`.
2. Open a row's "…" menu. The item is greyed out.
3. Click it.

Expected: nothing happens. Actual at `08dcec3c4b`: the browser opens
the address. The base did the same (round 2).

### Cause

reka-ui's `MenuItem` only skips its `select` event. (Round 1 said
headlessui's `MenuItem` prevented the click; round 2 found that it does
not reach a disabled item, as above.) The item
renders through `as-child` as `<a href>`, and `disabled` means nothing
on an `<a>`, so the browser follows it.

### Proposed fix

Not tried. In `DropdownActions.vue`, leave a disabled link action
without its address: `:href="action.disabled ? undefined : action.url"`.
This is what `f7a5fa9174` does.
