# A never-sorted chapter's authors swap places on "Create New Version" and on export and re-import, after pkp-lib#10570's tie-break

- **Severity** low
- **Effort** small
- **Kind** intention gap
- **Affects**
  - main: OMP at the PR head `94e167fe55` (pkp/omp#2523, not merged), and at its base
  - 3.5: OMP `stable-3_5_0` at `4aeacacc2a` (pkp/omp#2522 merged 2026-11-05), and before it
  - 3.4: OMP at the PR head `81d150199a` (pkp/omp#2521, not merged), and at its base
  - 3.3: not read
- **Introduced** not by the PR: the same swap happens at each base. Round 3 of the PRs (`orderBy('a.author_id')`, kaitlinnewson, 2026-11-04) makes the chapter's order steady, and the copy does not keep that order.

## Summary

Round 3 of the PRs adds bozana's tie-break. A chapter whose authors
all sit on the same chapter place (all `seq` 0, "never sorted") now
lists them by `author_id`, the order they were added in. The book page
reads that way on every line.

"Create New Version" and the native export then re-import do not keep
that order when the book's Contributors list puts those authors the
other way round. The copy writes the old places (0 and 0) again, and
the new authors get new ids in the Contributors order. The tie-break
then reads the new chapter in the Contributors order, the reverse of
the version it was copied from.

Book 14, chapter 2 ("Classical Music and the Classical Mind"), holds
Dawson and Dupuis, both on chapter place 0. Dupuis is first on the
Contributors list, and Dawson has the lower id:

| | old version (book page) | new version | export, then import |
|---|---|---|---|
| base, each line | Dawson, Dupuis | Dupuis, Dawson | Dupuis, Dawson |
| head, each line | Dawson, Dupuis | Dupuis, Dawson | Dupuis, Dawson |
| head + proposed fix | Dawson, Dupuis | Dawson, Dupuis | Dawson, Dupuis |

The issue asks that the copy and the export keep the chapter's own
order. They do for every sorted chapter (rounds 1 and 2, rechecked
today), and they do not for this one.

## Impact

- **Lost**: nothing. Two chapter authors swap places on the new
  version or on the imported book, and an editor can drag them back in
  the chapter's window.
- **Who**: OMP presses with chapters whose author rows tie. Such rows
  come from the native import of files that wrote tied `seq` values
  (the 3.4 and 3.5 export before this fix wrote `seq="0"` for every
  author when the Contributors list was never sorted), and from
  "Create New Version" before this fix. The swap needs one more thing:
  a Contributors list ordered against the authors' ids. PKP's datasets
  have no tied chapter rows (8 chapters with more than one author on
  each line, none tied).

## Steps to reproduce

1. On OMP main with pkp/omp#2523, as admin, give book 14's chapter 2
   two authors on the same chapter place (SQL, as an import leaves
   them): Dupuis and Dawson in `submission_chapter_authors` with `seq`
   0. Order the Contributors list Dupuis, then Dawson (drag, or `seq`
   0 and 1).
2. Open the book page: chapter 2 reads "Michael Dawson, Brian Dupuis".
3. As dbarnes, "Create New Version" on book 14.
4. The new version's chapter 2 reads Dupuis, Dawson (both on place 0
   again, new ids in the Contributors order).
5. Export book 14 (native XML) and import it: the imported chapter
   reads Dupuis, Dawson too. The file has both `<chapterAuthor>` in the
   right order, each with `seq="0"`.

**Expected**: the new version and the imported book read Dawson,
Dupuis, as the version they come from.

## Cause

`classes/publication/Repository.php` `version()` copies each chapter
author with `getChapterAuthorSequences()`, the old row's `seq`. The
export (`ChapterNativeXmlFilter::createChapterNode()`) writes the same
value. Tied values stay tied, and the tie is then broken by the new
`author_id`s. `PKP\publication\Repository::version()` assigns those
ids in the Contributors order, and the import assigns them in the
file's `<authors>` order.

## Proposed fix

Number the copies and the exported places in the order the chapter
reads (the collector's `seq`, then `author_id`). Tried on all three
heads: the swap is gone and the neighbours are unchanged. With it,
`getChapterAuthorSequences()` (DAO and Repository) has no caller left
and could go.
