Nothing outstanding; two questions.

The change: `Repo::user()->getEmailLocale($user, $context)` picks each
recipient's language: the journal's primary language when the user's
"Working Languages" include it or include none of the journal's
interface languages, otherwise the first working language the journal
offers. The "New Announcement" email (all three apps), the "Issue
Published Notify" email (OJS) and the editorial reminder use it per
recipient. Before, the announcement went out in the primary language and
the issue email in the publisher's interface language, to everyone. The
editorial reminder already did this, with one fault the helper fixes: an
editor whose working languages were German and French on an
English/French journal got no reminder (the job failed with a
TypeError, `array_intersect` kept the key `1`).

The drive: `shared/playwright/checks/sync/pkp-lib-13248/email-locale.js`
on OJS (both emails) and OMP (the announcement), a scratch journal with
English primary and French as a second interface language, a manager
working in French, six readers with working languages none, French,
French+English, German, German+French and English. At the PRs: none,
French+English, German and English get English, French and German+French
get French, the announcement and the issue email alike; an announcement
written in English only reaches the French readers as the French
template around the English title and summary. At the tips: every
announcement in English, every issue email in French (the publisher's
interface language).

## Questions

### 1. The links in a French email open the English pages

**Steps** (OJS, a fresh install)

1. A journal with English primary and French as a second interface
   language; a reader with "Français (Canada)" ticked alone under
   "Working Languages".
2. Add an announcement with "Send Email" ticked, or publish an issue with
   the email box ticked; let the jobs run.
   Observed: the French email's "l'annonce complète" link is
   `…/index.php/<journal>/en/announcement/view/1` (the issue email's
   `…/en/issue/view/3`); the unsubscribe and journal links are `/en/` too.

**Cause** `lib/pkp/classes/mail/mailables/AnnouncementNotify.php:86` and
OJS `classes/mail/variables/IssueEmailVariable.php:66` build the URL
without a locale, so it takes the queue worker's (the primary language),
not the mailable's. Every email does this; with one language for all
recipients it was hidden.

**Fix** pass `urlLocaleForPage: $locale` in both (the variable's
`values($locale)` hands it to `getIssueUrl()`):
`shared/playwright/checks/sync/pkp-lib-13248/fix-link-locale.diff`.
Tried at the PRs: the French readers' links become `/fr_CA/…`, the
others stay `/en/`. The unsubscribe and journal links
(`ContextEmailVariable`, the unsubscribe footer) would need the same.

### 2. A template customised in the primary language only

**Steps** (OJS, a fresh install)

1. The journal above; Settings › Workflow › Emails, "New Announcement":
   change the English subject to "JOURNAL NEWS: {$announcementTitle}" and
   add a line to the English body; leave the French tab alone.
2. Add an announcement with "Send Email" ticked; let the jobs run.
   Observed: a reader with no working language gets "JOURNAL NEWS: Call…"
   with the added line; a reader with French gets "Appel…" and the stock
   French text, without the journal's line. At the tips both got the
   journal's English text.

**Cause** `NewAnnouncementNotifyUsers::createMailable()` reads the
template in the recipient's language, and a language the journal never
edited holds the default text (`emailTemplate\DAO::fromRow`).

**Fix** none needed if intended (a journal edits each language's tab, as
it already must for the editorial reminder). Otherwise prefer the
primary language's text when only it was customised.

Check: `shared/playwright/checks/sync/pkp-lib-13248/custom-template.js`.
