## Summary

The `main` PRs carry the 3.5 change over unchanged, and with it the
3.5 regression: the accept page still re-reads the whole invitation when
ORCID comes back. Someone invited to a role who has no account skips
"Verify ORCID iD", fills in "Create OJS account" (username, password,
the privacy consent), goes back to the first step and connects their
ORCID iD after all. The page returns to "Create OJS account" still
showing the password and the ticked box, but it has forgotten both.
"Save and continue" refuses with "Please confirm that you have read and
agree to the privacy statement" under the ticked box. After unticking
and ticking it again, the page shows "The password field cannot be
null." under a field that still looks filled.

At the PR bases (the `main` tips) the same steps keep the password and
the consent, and "Save and continue" opens "Enter details".

The rest of the PR set does what the issue asks, as far as the walks
reach:

- An Assistant whom an editor lets change the publication can now use
  "Request verification" (the email arrives) and "Delete" (the iD is
  removed) on a contributor's ORCID iD, on OJS, OMP and OPS. At the
  bases both were refused. This fixes
  [pkp-e2e#750](https://github.com/jardakotesovec/pkp-e2e/issues/750)
  (spec U04 A5, the server's refusal).
- The three suites are green on CI at the PR heads (one OJS test
  flaky on an unrelated list order, below).

## Impact

- **Lost**: the password and privacy consent the invitee already
  entered. The screen keeps showing them, so the error messages
  contradict what the invitee sees.
- **Who**: new users accepting a role invitation in a journal, press or
  server with ORCID enabled, who skip ORCID at first and then go back
  for it. Every `main` install once the PRs merge, until this is fixed.
- **Way round**: on screen. Untick and tick the consent box again, and
  type the password again.

Medium: the invitee can still finish, but the page misleads them, and
only on a path a few invitees take.

## Steps to reproduce

Preconditions: OJS `main` with the PRs (OMP and OPS behave the same),
with an ORCID sandbox client. Mail is readable.

1. As the Journal Manager, Settings › Users & Roles › "ORCID": tick
   "Enable ORCID functionality", choose the public sandbox API, enter the
   sandbox client's ID and secret, and Save.
2. Users & Roles › "Users" › "Invite to a role": enter an email address
   that has no account, give it the "Reviewer" role with a start date,
   and send the invitation.
3. Signed out, open the email's link to accept. The page shows
   "STEP 1 - Verify ORCID iD".
4. Press "Skip ORCID verification".
5. On "STEP 2 - Create OJS account", fill in "Username" and "Password",
   tick "Yes, I agree to have my data collected and stored according to
   the Privacy Statement", and press "Save and continue". The page shows
   "STEP 3 - Enter details".
6. Press "Back", then "Back" again, to return to "STEP 1 - Verify ORCID
   iD".
7. Press "Verify ORCID iD", sign in to the ORCID sandbox in the window
   that opens, and authorize. The window closes and the page shows
   "STEP 2 - Create OJS account", with the username, the password (as
   dots) and the ticked box.
8. Press "Save and continue".

**Expected** (as at the `main` tip): the page moves on to "STEP 3 -
Enter details".

**Observed**: the page stays on step 2 and shows "Please confirm that
you have read and agree to the privacy statement" under the ticked box.
Untick the box, tick it again, and press "Save and continue": the page
shows "The password field cannot be null." under the password field,
which still shows its dots.

## Cause

The same as on 3.5. `AcceptInvitationVerifyOrcid.vue`'s `orcidAdded()`
handles the callback's `orcidAddedToInvitation` event by calling the
store's `receiveInvitation()`, the page-load initializer, which resets
the account fields along with reading the ORCID iD back
(`AcceptInvitationPageStore.js` at `06ebe4d4ed`, lines 96-104, comments left out):

```js
updateAcceptInvitationPayload('privacyStatement', userId.value ? true : false);
updateAcceptInvitationPayload('username', data.value.username);
updateAcceptInvitationPayload('password', null);
```

By the code (not walked), the same call also resets the country, and the
name and affiliation fields whenever the server holds a value, so what
was typed on "Enter details" and not yet saved goes the same way.

## Proposed fix

The 3.5 report's: after ORCID, re-read only what ORCID changed (for
example a small `refreshOrcid()` in the store that fetches the
invitation and updates `orcid` and `orcidIsVerified` alone), in both
`stable-3_5_0` and `main`. Not tried.
