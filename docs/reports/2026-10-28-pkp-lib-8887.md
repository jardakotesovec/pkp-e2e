# Section editors and assistants have no "Declined" view, so their declined submissions vanish from the dashboard

- **Severity** medium
- **Effort** small
- **Kind** regression
- **Affects**
  - main: OJS, OMP, OPS
  - 3.5: OJS, OMP, OPS
  - 3.4: none (code; the "Archived" tab lists an assigned editor's declined submissions)
  - 3.3: none (code; its "Archives" tab lists them the same way)
  - ore: OJS (code; pkp-lib `ore` has the same role list)
- **Introduced** `pkp/pkp-lib#9469` for `pkp/pkp-lib#8887` · [aafa2d56e5](https://github.com/pkp/pkp-lib/commit/aafa2d56e5e0402ddf100d04465e3aa77a0279c9), [0a492918f1](https://github.com/pkp/pkp-lib/commit/0a492918f17947db19770f1bf6654c37e81b8e47) · merged 2023-11-13, first released in 3.5.0 · Vitalii Bezsheiko (Vitaliy-1)

- **Tracked in** spec U23 [A1](https://github.com/jardakotesovec/pkp-e2e/blob/main/docs/specs/U23-submissions-dashboard.md#a1) · ci-triage "Open regressions" · Temporary: delete once acted on

## Summary

A section editor (a press's series editor, a server's moderator) or an
assistant such as a Funding coordinator can be assigned to a submission
that is then declined. Once that happens, the submission disappears from
every view of their editorial dashboard. Their side menu has no
"Declined" entry. A journal manager's has one, and lists the
submission there.

Nothing is lost, and the submission's workflow still opens from a link
or its address. On `main` the side menu's "Search submissions" box finds
it by title. On 3.5 there is no such box, so the dashboard cannot bring
the submission back at all. Until 3.4, the dashboard's "Archived" tab
listed these declined submissions for the editors assigned to them.

## Impact

- **Lost**: the way back to a declined submission (to read it, answer
  the author, or revert the decline). Nobody is told: the decision's own
  confirmation says the submission was "sent to the archives", and no
  archive exists on these users' dashboards.
- **Who**: every section editor, series editor, moderator and assistant,
  for each submission they are assigned to that gets declined. A
  journal's ordinary flow declines many submissions.
- **Way round**: on `main`, "Search submissions" by title or ID. On 3.5,
  only the workflow's address, a notification, or an email link. A
  manager can still find the submission and pass it on.

Medium: the submissions are intact, and on `main` a search finds them.
But on 3.5 the dashboard gives the people assigned to them no way back.
It would be high if the 3.5 line were rated alone.

## Steps to reproduce

Preconditions: PKP's default dataset, OJS `main` (and `stable-3_5_0`).
Submission 18, "Self-Organization in Multi-Level Institutions in
Networked Environments", is declined in the Submission stage, and
section editors `dbuskins` and `sberardo` are assigned to it. For the
assistant, these steps add a Funding coordinator to the same
submission:

- As `admin`: Administration › "Hosted Journals" › "Settings wizard" ›
  "Users" › `svogt` › "Edit User": tick "Funding coordinator", "OK".
- As `dbarnes`: open submission 18, "Participants" › "Assign": role
  "Funding coordinator", "Search", "Sarah Vogt", "OK".

(OMP: decline book 6, "The Information Literacy User's Guide", as
`dbarnes` from its Internal Review stage; `dbuskins` is its series
editor. OPS: preprint 4, "Genetic transformation of forest trees", is
declined with moderator `dbuskins` assigned. A preprint server has no
assistant role that can join a submission, so OPS has no assistant
step.)

1. Sign in as `dbarnes`, open the editorial dashboard
   (`/index.php/publicknowledge/en/dashboard/editorial`), and look at
   the side menu.
2. Sign out, sign in as `dbuskins`, and open the same page.
3. Open each view in the side menu and look for submission 18.
4. On `main`: type "Self-Organization" into "Search submissions" and
   press Enter.
5. Repeat 2-4 as `svogt`.

**Expected**: like `dbarnes`, `dbuskins` and `svogt` get a "Declined"
entry listing the declined submission they are assigned to, and only
that one. This matches what 3.4's "Archived" tab listed for them.

**Observed**: `dbarnes`'s side menu ends with "Published 2" and
"Declined 1", and "Declined (1)" lists submission 18. The side menus of
`dbuskins` and `svogt` end at "Published" with no "Declined" entry, and
none of their views lists submission 18:

```
dbuskins: Assigned to me 15 · Active submissions 15 · All in submission stage 5 · … · Scheduled for publication 0 · Published 2
svogt:    Assigned to me 3 · Active submissions 3 · … · Scheduled for publication 0 · Published 1
```

On `main`, step 4 shows "Search Results (1)" with submission 18 for
both of them. 3.5 has no "Search submissions" box. OMP and OPS read the
same on both lines. Control: `minoue`, a section editor not assigned to
submission 18, finds nothing in step 4 ("Search Results (0)"). With the
fix, minoue's "Declined" must stay empty.

## Cause

`PKP\submission\Repository::mapDashboardViews()` (lib/pkp
`classes/submission/Repository.php`, line 1290 on `main`, 1120 on
`stable-3_5_0`) gives each dashboard view the list of roles that get it,
and `filterViewsByUserRoles()` drops a view for any user with none of
those roles. `TYPE_DECLINED` lists only
`ROLE_ID_SITE_ADMIN, ROLE_ID_MANAGER, ROLE_ID_AUTHOR`. Every other view
that lists finished work ("Scheduled for publication", "Published")
also lists `ROLE_ID_SUB_EDITOR` and `ROLE_ID_ASSISTANT`.

The view's collector is already written for them. When the user cannot
see unassigned submissions, it narrows to their own assignments
(`assignedTo([$user->getId()], $assignedWithRoles)`) and uses the
`assigned` op, which the backend `_submissions/assigned` endpoint serves
to sub-editors and assistants. Only the role list keeps the view from
them.

The view was created manager-only in
[aafa2d56e5](https://github.com/pkp/pkp-lib/commit/aafa2d56e5e0402ddf100d04465e3aa77a0279c9),
and [0a492918f1](https://github.com/pkp/pkp-lib/commit/0a492918f17947db19770f1bf6654c37e81b8e47)
added the author, both in `pkp/pkp-lib#9469`. The issue that PR
implements, `pkp/pkp-lib#8887`, lists "Declined" among the views and
says: "For users with the `SUB_EDITOR` or `ASSISTANT` role, they will
only see submissions they are assigned to in one of those roles." Only
"Assigned to Me" is named as an exception there; the PR's discussion
gives no reason for leaving editors out of "Declined". The 3.4
dashboard's "Archived" tab (lib/pkp `stable-3_4_0`
`pages/dashboard/DashboardHandler.php`) listed declined, published and
scheduled submissions for every role, narrowed to the user's own
assignments for non-managers.

Reach:

- The side menu, its counts (`getDashboardViews(…, true)` in
  `PKPBackendSubmissionsController`) and the template's menu
  (`PKPTemplateManager`) all read the same view list, so the one role
  list hides the entry everywhere. Code, and walked on screen.
- All three apps use the shared view. OPS's
  `APP\submission\Repository::mapDashboardViews()` keeps
  `TYPE_DECLINED` in its own list. Walked.
- `main`'s "Search Results" view (`getSearchView()`, added for
  `pkp/pkp-lib#12932`) has no status filter, so it finds declined
  submissions for these roles. 3.5 lacks it. Walked.
- No stored data is affected.

## Proposed fix

Give the "Declined" view the same editorial roles as "Published":

```diff
                     return new DashboardView(
                         $key,
                         __('submission.dashboard.view.declined'),
-                        [Role::ROLE_ID_SITE_ADMIN, Role::ROLE_ID_MANAGER, Role::ROLE_ID_AUTHOR],
+                        [Role::ROLE_ID_SITE_ADMIN, Role::ROLE_ID_MANAGER, Role::ROLE_ID_SUB_EDITOR, Role::ROLE_ID_ASSISTANT, Role::ROLE_ID_AUTHOR],
```

([fix.diff](https://github.com/jardakotesovec/pkp-e2e/blob/main/shared/playwright/checks/sync/pkp-lib-8887/fix.diff),
which applies to `stable-3_5_0` and `ore` as it stands, at a line
offset.) The view already
narrows to the user's own assignments, so nothing else changes. A
section editor sees only the declined submissions they are assigned
to. This is the rule `pkp/pkp-lib#8887` states, and what 3.4's
"Archived" tab showed.

The fix was tried on `main` on all three apps. `dbuskins` and the
Funding coordinator `svogt` each got "Declined 1" listing their declined
submission. A section editor not assigned to it (`minoue`, OMP
`sberardo`) got "Declined 0" and no row, and `dbarnes`'s "Declined (1)"
was unchanged.

**Alternatives**:

- Rely on `main`'s "Search submissions": it finds the submission only
  if the user remembers its title or ID, and it does not reach 3.5.
- A separate "My declined" view: a new pattern, when the view already
  scopes itself.

**What goes with it**: a backport to `stable-3_5_0` (the same line, at
line 1120), and to `ore` if the team wants it there. For the test, a
case in the dashboard's role-scope tests: a section editor assigned to
a declined submission sees "Declined (1)" with it, and one not assigned
sees no row (U23 scenario 11 in this campaign).

Small: one line in the shared repository class, plus a test.
