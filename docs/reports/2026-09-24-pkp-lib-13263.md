# Review Details names an older "Reviewer Thanked" date as the latest step after a review is reverted and completed again

Intention gap, with one translation regression and one open question
beside it. Found in a PR review of the unmerged pkp/pkp-lib#13346 and
pkp/ui-library#987 (issue pkp/pkp-lib#13263; ojs#5835 bumps the
pointers). Driven on OJS at the PR heads (lib/pkp `26a5efcb74`,
ui-library `ad0fdd33`), each merged locally onto the current tips
(lib/pkp `25182919bf`, ui-library `1afd40a9`). OMP carries the same code
(its History and Review Details are shared) but was not driven for
these findings. OPS has no review stage. Recorded in U27 as
[A33](../specs/U27-reviewer-assignment-and-management.md#a33),
[A34](../specs/U27-reviewer-assignment-and-management.md#a34) and
[A35](../specs/U27-reviewer-assignment-and-management.md#a35) on the
companion branch `i13263`, and in the `docs/tracking/ci-triage.md`
companion row. Temporary: delete once acted on.

## Summary

1. **A33 (intention gap, minor).** The issue asks Review Details to
   "always show the latest relevant step". Take a review that was
   thanked, taken back with "Revert Decision", then marked complete
   again. The window reads "Reviewer Thanked: {date of the first
   thank}". On the same review, History lists the newer "Review
   Completed" after that thank. Reaching the second completion through
   a review decision's "Notify Reviewers" step does the same. That path
   also sends the reviewer a second "Thank you for your review", and its
   date shows on neither surface.
2. **A34 (regression, minor).** The new key
   `editor.review.reviewerReminded` exists only in English. Every other
   interface language shows "##editor.review.reviewerReminded##" on the
   reminder line, where French read "Rappel" before. The usual
   translation lag, reported because a translated screen regresses.
3. **A35 (question).** After "Revert Decision", History drops the
   "Reviewer Thanked" line even though the thank-you email went out. The
   PR does this on purpose, and the Review Details window agrees with
   it. The issue, though, says History "shows all the dates we track".
   Is this intended? Our lean is yes.

Everything else matches the issue on OJS, in every state: invited,
reminded, declined, accepted, submitted, completed, thanked, and reverted
from complete. Both surfaces show the new labels, and History uses the
"{Label}: {date}" form. No request failed and the console had no errors.

## Impact

A33 and A35 need an editor to take back a review and complete it again,
which is uncommon. The window then misstates which step came last. It
also hides that the reviewer was thanked a second time. Nothing is lost:
History still lists every stored date, in order. A34 affects every
non-English editor who opens History or Review Details for a reminded
reviewer, until the next translation update.

## Steps to reproduce

Preconditions: a fresh OJS install. A submission in review with one
reviewer who has accepted and submitted a review with a recommendation.
An editor assigned to the submission.

**A33, by Mark as Complete**

1. As the editor, open the reviewer's "Read Review" and press "Mark as
   Complete". Close the window.
2. Press "Thank Reviewer" and send the email (T1).
3. Press "Revert Decision" and confirm with "Unconsider this Review".
4. Wait a minute. Open "Read Review" again and press "Mark as Complete"
   (T2).
5. Read the window's dated line, then open the row's More Actions ›
   "History".

**Expected** (issue: "it always shows the latest relevant step"):
Review Details reads "Review Completed: {T2}".

**Observed** (2026-09-24, T1 06:27, T2 06:29):

- Review Details: `Reviewer Thanked: 2026-09-24 06:27 AM`.
- History:

  ```
  Request Sent: 2026-09-24 06:27 AM
  Request Accepted: 2026-09-24 06:27 AM
  Review Submitted: 2026-09-24 06:27 AM
  Reviewer Thanked: 2026-09-24 06:27 AM
  Review Completed: 2026-09-24 06:29 AM
  ```

- The row reads "Reviewer Thanked".

**A33, by a decision's "Notify Reviewers"**

Do steps 1 to 3 above. Then wait a minute and record "Request
Revisions": go through "Notify Authors" and "Notify Reviewers" with the
reviewer ticked, then "Record Decision" (T3).

- **Observed:** the reviewer's mailbox holds a second "Thank you for your
  review" at T3. Review Details still reads "Reviewer Thanked: {T1}".
  History ends "Reviewer Thanked: {T1}" / "Review Completed: {T3}". The
  window's own `GET …/reviewAssignments/{id}` reads `considered: 2`,
  `dateConsidered` T3 and `dateAcknowledged` still T1.
- **Control:** in the same decision, a reviewer thanked for the first
  time reads correctly. Review Details shows "Reviewer Thanked: {T3}",
  and History ends "Review Completed: {T3}" / "Reviewer Thanked: {T3}".

**A35**

Do steps 1 to 3 above, then open History.

- **Before the revert:** Request Sent, Request Accepted, Review
  Submitted, Review Completed and Reviewer Thanked.
- **After the revert:**

  ```
  Request Sent: 2026-09-24 06:27 AM
  Request Accepted: 2026-09-24 06:27 AM
  Review Submitted: 2026-09-24 06:27 AM
  ```

  The thank-you email is in the mailbox, and the API still carries
  `dateAcknowledged`. Before this change, History showed "{date}
  Acknowledged" in this state. Review Details reads "Review Submitted:
  …" after the revert, so the two surfaces agree.

**A34**

1. On a journal with Français (Canada) as an interface language, send a
   reminder to a reviewer who has not answered ("Send Reminder").
2. Switch the interface to Français.
3. Open that reviewer's History and Review Details.

- **Observed:**
  - History: `Demande envoyée : 2026-09-24 06:15 AM` /
    `##editor.review.reviewerReminded## : 2026-09-24 06:15 AM`.
  - Review Details: `##editor.review.reviewerReminded## : 2026-09-24
    06:15 a.m.`
  - The other new labels are translated ("Demande acceptée",
    "Évaluation soumise", "Évaluateur-trice remercié-e").
- **Before:** "{date} Rappel".

## Cause

- **A33:** `ReviewDetailsInfo.vue` `latestActivity` returns the first
  non-empty entry of a fixed list. Thanked (when not unconsidered) comes
  first, ahead of the date-bearing Completed, so the older of two dates
  wins. Two server-side details feed it:
  - `ReviewAssignmentController::markReviewConsidered` sets `considered`
    to RECONSIDERED and a new `dateConsidered`, but leaves
    `dateAcknowledged` alone.
  - `NotifyReviewers::sendReviewersEmail` sets `dateAcknowledged` only
    `if (!$reviewAssignment->getDateAcknowledged())`.
- **A35:** `PKPReviewerGridHandler::reviewHistory` maps
  `editor.review.reviewerThanked` to `null` while
  `getConsidered() == REVIEW_ASSIGNMENT_UNCONSIDERED`. `unconsiderReview`
  nulls `dateConsidered`, so both lines go.
- **A34:** `editor.review.reviewerReminded` is added to
  `locale/en/editor.po` only. `Locale::translate` has no English
  fallback.

## Proposed fix

A proposal; the team decides.

- **A33:** in `latestActivity`, among the entries with a date, pick the
  latest date rather than the first entry in the list, keeping the
  current order only to break ties. That makes a newer Completed win
  over an older Thanked. Alternatively, `NotifyReviewers` could refresh
  `dateAcknowledged` on every thank it sends, so that the second
  thank's date is recorded.
- **A34:** nothing before merge, if the team accepts the translation
  lag. Otherwise reuse an already-translated key for the label until
  Weblate catches up.
- **A35:** confirm the intent. If History should keep every tracked
  date, drop the `UNCONSIDERED ? null :` guard in `reviewHistory` only.
