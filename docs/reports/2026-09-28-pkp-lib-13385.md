# A message typed in the Participants panel still fails on a press's Internal Review, and a message the recipient may not receive goes out under another name

Intention gaps (Findings 1 and 2). OJS, OMP and OPS at
ojs `72b85f4ba0`, omp `3cd59e9443`, ops `e2111e3aae` with
pkp/pkp-lib#13385 (head `cf72cc78d8`, open, 2026-09-25) merged onto their
lib/pkp (`26ae6431b5` / `17a1f01fed`), before its merge; issue
pkp/pkp-lib#12593. stable-3_5_0: not affected (the discussion-template
migration is `main` only). Tracked in the stage-participants spec (U35
A3 and the new entries the PR review added) and the ci-triage companion
row `i12593_missing_template`. Temporary: delete once acted on.

Update 2026-09-28: @jarda.kotesovec ruled on Finding 2 in the thread: a
template's role limit restricts who is offered and may use it, never who
receives it. Finding 2 is therefore an intention gap, and its fix is to
stop checking the recipient (option 1 below).

## Summary

The PR fixes both failures reported on the issue on 2026-09-24: a
message typed with the predefined-message list left blank is now sent
(as the stage's "Discussion (…)"), and templates added in Settings can
be chosen and sent. Two cases remain. **Finding 1:** where the stage has
no "Discussion (…)" template to fall back on (every press's Internal
Review, and any stage whose "Discussion (…)" template a manager deleted),
the same typed message still fails on the server: the window stays open,
nothing is sent, and on "Assign" the person is assigned anyway.
**Finding 2:** a template limited to roles the recipient does not hold is
sent anyway, under the stage's "Discussion (…)" name, without telling the
sender; a letter such as "Request Copyedit" then reaches the wrong person
under a misleading subject, with a raw placeholder in the discussion and
without its task.

## Finding 1 — No "Discussion (…)" template to fall back on: the message fails on the server

### Impact

Every press editor or manager who writes to someone from the Internal
Review stage's Participants panel: that stage offers no predefined
message at all, so typing one is the only way to send, and it always
fails. On a journal the same happens on any stage whose "Discussion (…)"
template was deleted under Settings › Workflow › "Tasks and
Discussions", which that screen allows. The typed message is lost; on
"Assign" the person is assigned without their message and without a
reason on screen, so the editor may believe it went out. Workaround:
open a discussion from the stage's discussions panel instead. Major:
messages from a whole workflow stage of every press are lost, and the
app fails on the server each time.

### Steps to reproduce

Preconditions:
- A fresh OMP install with a press, its Press Manager, a Series editor
  and an author.
- A submission sent to "Internal Review".

1. Sign in as the Press Manager and open the submission's "Internal
   Review" stage.
2. In "Participants", open the author's row menu and choose "Notify".
3. Leave "Choose a predefined message to use, or fill out the form
   below." on its blank entry (the list has no other entry here).
4. Type "Please check the reviewer comments." in "Message".
5. Press "Notify".
6. Repeat with "Assign": choose the Series editor, leave the list blank,
   type a message, press "OK".

**Expected** (issue #12593, 2026-09-25 comment: "it fallbacks to the
title of the default task template if it's not set"): the message is
sent, as it now is on the Submission, Review, Copyediting and Production
stages.

**Observed:** "Notify" stays open as filled; "OK" on "Assign" stays open
as filled, yet the Series editor is assigned (the row appears after a
reload). No email, no discussion, no notice. Both requests answer 500:

```
PHP Fatal error:  Uncaught Error: Call to a member function promote() on null in lib/pkp/controllers/grid/users/stageParticipant/form/PKPStageParticipantNotifyForm.php:194
```

The same on OJS after deleting "Discussion (Submission)" under Settings
› Workflow › "Tasks and Discussions" (row menu › "Delete"), then
"Notify" or "Assign" from the Submission stage with the list blank.
Control: on the Submission stage of a journal or press with its
"Discussion (Submission)" in place, the same steps send the message.

### Cause

`PKPStageParticipantNotifyForm::sendMessage()` falls back to
`Template::withKeys(Repo::editorialTask()->getDiscussionTemplateKeys(), $contextId)->withStageId($this->_stageId)->withType(DISCUSSION)->first()`
and calls `$template->promote($submission)` without checking the result.
OMP's `registry/taskTemplates.xml` installs no template for
`WORKFLOW_STAGE_ID_INTERNAL_REVIEW`, and the four
`DISCUSSION_NOTIFICATION_*` keys cover stages 1, 3, 4 and 5 only; a
deleted default leaves its stage with nothing either. The fallback also
runs for a template the recipient may not receive (Finding 2), so that
case fails the same way on these stages.

### Proposed fix

A proposal; the team decides.
1. Guard the null: when no template is found, send without one (the
   mailable from the typed message, the discussion titled with a
   generic, localized string such as the stage's name), so every stage
   can send. Covers deleted defaults and Internal Review.
2. Additionally, or instead for Internal Review only: install a
   "Discussion (Internal Review)" template for OMP (a
   `DISCUSSION_NOTIFICATION_INTERNAL_REVIEW` key in the registry, the
   install and the #12593 migration). Does not cover a deleted default.
3. At the least, refuse with a form error instead of the 500, so the
   editor knows nothing was sent.

## Finding 2 — A template limited to roles is checked against the recipient and sent under another name

### Impact

Editors and managers who limit templates to roles under Settings ›
Workflow › "Tasks and Discussions". The list shows templates by the
sender's roles (a manager sees them all), but the check at sending is
made against the recipient's. A template the recipient does not qualify
for is not refused: the typed text goes out as the stage's "Discussion
(…)", with the same "Notification sent to users." notice, so the sender
cannot tell their choice was replaced. For a letter template the effect
is misleading: the recipient gets its full text under a generic subject,
the discussion shows an unfilled placeholder, and the template's task
(for "Request Copyedit", the copyedit assignment) is not raised; had it
gone to someone qualified, it would have been. Minor: the message
arrives and nothing is lost, but it arrives mislabelled and the sender
is not told.

### Steps to reproduce

Preconditions:
- A fresh OJS install with a journal, its Journal Manager, a copyeditor
  and an author.
- Under Settings › Workflow › "Tasks and Discussions", the Copyediting
  stage's "Request Copyedit" edited and limited to the Copyeditor role,
  then saved.
- A submission in the Copyediting stage, the author on it.

1. Sign in as the Journal Manager and open the submission's
   "Copyediting" stage.
2. In "Participants", open the author's row menu and choose "Notify".
3. Choose "Request Copyedit" in "Choose a predefined message…" ("Message"
   fills with "Dear NAME, A new submission is ready to be copyedited…").
4. Press "Notify".
5. Open the author's email and the new discussion in the stage's
   discussions panel.

**Expected:** "Request Copyedit" goes out as chosen: the role limit
decides who may pick the template (the Journal Manager may), not who
receives it (ruling above).

**Observed:** "Notification sent to users."; the author's email has the
subject "Discussion (Copyediting)" and the copyedit request's full text
("Dear Ava Author, A new submission is ready to be copyedited: … Please
follow these steps to complete this task: …"); the discussion is
"Discussion (Copyediting)" and its first entry reads
"Dear {$recipientName},"; the author gets no copyedit task. Control: the
same template sent to the copyeditor arrives as "Request Copyedit"
("Dear Cora Copy,") and raises the copyedit task. The swap without a
letter template: a template limited to an editor role sent to the author
(from a manager, or from an assigned Section editor whose list offers
it) arrives as "Discussion (Submission)" with the typed text, on all
three apps.

### Cause

`sendMessage()` checks `Repo::editorialTask()->isTemplateAccessibleToUser($template, $user)`
with `$user` the recipient, while `PKPStageParticipantNotifyForm::fetch()`
and `StageParticipantGridHandler::fetchTemplateBody()` filter by the
signed-in sender. When the recipient check fails, the PR replaces the
template with the stage's `DISCUSSION_NOTIFICATION_*` one; the typed
body stays, the subject, discussion title and key become the fallback's,
so the head note is compiled with `getEmailVariableNames()` of the
fallback key (no `recipientName`) and the `switch ($templateKey)` misses
`COPYEDIT_REQUEST`.

### Proposed fix

A proposal; the ruling above settles its direction.
1. Drop the recipient check in `sendMessage()`: the sender was already
   limited by `fetch()`'s list; at most re-check the signed-in user
   (`$request->getUser()`) against the posted template, as
   `fetchTemplateBody()` does, and send the template as chosen. The
   fallback to "Discussion (…)" then serves only a blank or missing
   template (Finding 1).
2. The recipient-based manager bypass the PR adds to
   `isTemplateAccessibleToUser()` becomes unnecessary for this path.
