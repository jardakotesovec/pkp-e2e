<a id="finding-1"></a>
## Finding 1: "Invitation Sent" still promises an answer by notification or email

The app PRs reword the dialog a manager gets after sending an
invitation: "{email} has been invited to a new role in OJS. You can see
the user's response on the Users & Roles page, in your OJS
notifications, or in your email." The grammar is right now, and the
promise is the one the old sentence made: when the person accepts or
declines, no notification arrives and no email is sent. The invitation's
row leaves the "Invitations" table, and only an acceptance shows, as a
new role under Current Users.

Read again at the new heads with the walk of
[pkp-e2e#515](https://github.com/jardakotesovec/pkp-e2e/issues/515), on
the three applications: `rvaca` invites two members as Copyeditor, one
accepts and one declines; afterwards "Tasks" is as it was and `rvaca`'s
mailbox is empty.

The diff gives the sentence pkp-e2e#515 proposes: "{$email} has been
invited to a new role in OJS. The invitation is listed under
Invitations on the Users & Roles page while it awaits their answer."
With it the dialog reads so on the three applications. The wording is
the team's; sending the updates instead is the larger alternative that
report names.

<a id="finding-2"></a>
## Finding 2: two words in the same files

Step 3 of "Invite to a role" says "…next steps, journal GDPR polices and
ORCID verification" (`userInvitation.sendMail.stepDescription`). With
ORCID switched on, the accept page's first step says "If you chose to
skip it now, you can verify your ORCID iD from your profile section in
OJS later" (`acceptInvitation.verifyOrcid.stepDescription`). Both were
read on screen at the new heads, on the three applications and both
branches. They are what is left of
[pkp-e2e#540](https://github.com/jardakotesovec/pkp-e2e/issues/540).

With the diff they read "GDPR policies" and "If you choose to skip it
now".
