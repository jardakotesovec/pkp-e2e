## Summary

The PRs add DataCite export and deposit for public peer reviews. A
review's DataCite record validates against DataCite's schema. An
anonymous or double-anonymous reviewer shows only as "unknown". An open
reviewer shows only their name, a verified ORCID and an affiliation.
Nothing that worked before broke: the suites are green with one
intended test change.

Round 1 reported five findings. At round 3 (the latest heads):

- **Open, finding 3, narrowed**: on a journal whose primary language
  is not English, a review of a work with no English title still gets no
  DataCite record. "Export DOIs" and the deposit job stop with "titles
  are required but none were found". For any such work, on any journal,
  the Crossref review record goes out with an empty `<title/>`.
  Round 3 fixed the English-journal half: round 2's crash
  (`Journal::getContext()`) is gone, and those works' reviews export
  and deposit again.
- **Fixed**: 1 (test mode sends the reviews; round 2), 4 (the URL
  carries a plain `&`; round 2) and 5 (the "Updated" date is written;
  round 2). All three still hold at round 3.
- **Closed, intended**: 2. DataCite does not support DOI versioning, so
  a review is registered as reviewing the current version (the team's
  ruling, 2026-10-28).

## Finding 1 — With DataCite "Testing" on, a deposit never sent the work's reviews (fixed)

Fixed at round 2 (lib/pkp `b9bc59e211`): `DepositSubmission` now also
sends the reviews when the agency's `testMode` setting is on and the
article DOI reads "Submitted". Walked: in test mode the four reviews go
to `mds.test.datacite.org` right after the article, and a refused article
deposit ("Error") still queues none. Round 1 found it at lib/pkp
`6fe60a84ef`.

## Finding 2 — With "DOI Versioning" on, a review of an earlier version is registered as reviewing the current one (closed: intended)

Closed 2026-10-28 on the team's ruling in the review thread: DataCite
does not support DOI versioning, so reviews are intentionally registered
as reviews of the current version. Unchanged at round 2: the record names
the current version's title and `Reviews` its DOI, while the URL points
at the reviewed version. Under DataCite with versioning, a review of an
earlier version is still never deposited, because that version's DOI
never reads "Registered" (U45 OJS8). This follows the same ruling.

## Finding 3 — A review of a work with no English title cannot be exported or deposited; Crossref gives it an empty title (open)

- **Severity** medium
- **Effort** small
- **Kind** intention gap
- **Crash** server
- **Affects**
  - main: OJS (walked)

### Impact

- **Lost**: on a journal whose primary language is not English, the
  DataCite review record of a work with no English title. "Export
  DOIs" answers a server error ("DataCite export: titles are required
  but none were found."). The deposit job fails, is retried, then lands
  in Failed Jobs, and the review DOI stays "Submitted". For Crossref, on
  any journal, such a work's review record goes out with an empty
  `<title/>` (before these PRs it went out with the placeholder
  `##plugins.importexport.crossref.reviewTitle##`). Round 2 had made the
  DataCite record fail on English journals too ("Call to undefined
  method Journal::getContext()"); round 3 fixed that.
- **Who**: every journal publishing works titled in a language whose
  locale file lacks the new `submission.doi.review.title` key, which
  is every language but English until translations arrive, and any
  language the translators never cover.
- **Way round**: add an English title to the work.

Medium: a server error on export and in a background job, for every journal published in a language other than English.

### Steps to reproduce

Preconditions: a journal whose primary language is French, DataCite
chosen, DOIs on for "Articles" and "Peer Review". A work with a French
title only has a public, completed, editor-confirmed review with a
DOI, and is published.

1. DOIs page: "Deposit DOIs" for the work. Let the jobs run (`php
   lib/pkp/tools/jobs.php run`, or the job runner).
2. The article is registered. The review's `DepositPeerReview` job fails
   with `Exception: DataCite export: titles are required but none were
   found.` (`DataciteXmlFilter.php:735`), and the review's DOI stays
   "Submitted".

"Export DOIs" for the same work fails the same way. With Crossref as the
agency, the review's record carries `<titles><title/></titles>`, on an
English journal too.

### Cause

`ReviewAssignment\Repository::getReviewTitle()` translates with
`Locale::getBundle($locale)->translateSingular()`, which returns `null`
for a locale that lacks the key, and `submission.doi.review.title`
exists in English only. `DataciteXmlFilter::createTitlesNode()` then
falls back to the journal's primary locale, which since round 3 it reads
correctly as `$deployment->getContext()->getPrimaryLocale()`. On a
French-primary journal that asks for French again, gets `null` again,
and the filter throws. Crossref's filter passes the `null` title to
`htmlspecialchars()`.

### Proposed fix

Fall back to English in `getReviewTitle()`
(`checks/sync/pkp-lib-13119/fix-pkp-lib.diff`, its `Repository.php`
part). That covers the DataCite fallback and Crossref at once:

```php
return Locale::getBundle($locale)->translateSingular('submission.doi.review.title', $params)
    ?? Locale::getBundle(LocaleInterface::DEFAULT_LOCALE)->translateSingular('submission.doi.review.title', $params);
```

Tried on the round-3 heads: the French-primary journal's record builds
as `Review: "Relevé des vasières …" (Round 1, Review 1)` and validates.
Crossref writes the same titles for both French works. The comment above
`getReviewTitle()`, which promises `null` for a missing translation,
would need updating. If the team would rather keep that promise, the
English fallback can sit in both filters instead.

## Finding 4 — The review's landing URL was registered with DataCite as `&amp;reviewId=` (fixed)

Fixed at round 2 (ojs `d3a6961cb6`: `_getObjectUrl()` passes `$escape =
false` for the review URL). Walked: DataCite's `/doi` call carries
`…/version/4?tab=peer-review-record&reviewId=6`.

## Finding 5 — A review record never carried its "Updated" date (fixed)

Fixed at round 2 (ojs `d3a6961cb6`: `if ($lastModified)`). Walked:
every review record carries `Updated`, and still validates against
kernel-4.
