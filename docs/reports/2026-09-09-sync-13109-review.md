### 2b. Intention gap with a visible consequence — U40 A16 (new 🐞)

Register entry A16 in `docs/specs/U40-publication-metadata.md`, footnote
f-a16. Driven on all three apps by the claim checker
(`.reports/U40/cc-K1.md` block K1-1; `.reports/U40/ccK1/au-v2-summary-*`,
`k1-1-401-body-ojs.json`); script `shared/playwright/checks/U40/K1/k1.js`
and the follow-up `k1-401-body.js`.

Exact steps (OJS scratch journal on a fresh install at the tips above):

1. An Author submits an article (scratch author, default Author role).
2. As the Journal Manager: open the submission's workflow; on the
   Participants panel open the Author's "Edit Assignment", tick "Allow this
   person to make changes to the publication…", OK. (Ticking after step 3
   gives the same result.)
3. Journal Manager: Publication › "Publish"; the scratch journal has no
   issue, so the confirm reads "…will be published immediately without any
   issue association…". Header reads "Status: Published"; the submission
   now rests in the Done stage.
4. Journal Manager: Publication › "Create New Version" → "Version of Record
   1.1", "Status: Unpublished".
5. Sign in as the Author, My Submissions › the submission › Publication ›
   "Version of Record 1.1" › Title & Abstract: no banner, Save enabled.
   Change the title, press Save.
6. Observed: "Saving", then about 1.5 s later the toast "An unexpected error
   has occurred. Please reload the page and try again."; the form footer
   stays empty; the edit is gone on reload. The browser's request
   (`X-Http-Method-Override: PUT` to
   `api/v1/submissions/{id}/publications/{newId}`) answers **401**
   `{"error":"user.authorization.accessibleWorkflowStage","errorMessage":"You
   don't currently have access to that stage of the workflow."}`.
7. Control: Journal Manager unpublishes 1.0 → the Author's Save on 1.1 is
   accepted (200) and persists. On a journal whose 1.0 is only scheduled the
   Author's Save on 1.1 is accepted too.

Identical on OMP ("Version of Record 1.1") and OPS ("Author Original 1.1").

Why (the developer layer, footnote f-a16): after publishing, the submission
rests in stage 6 (Done). The write's stage policy
(`UserAccessibleWorkflowStageRequiredPolicy`) needs the Author's group to
hold that stage. The PR grants stage 6 to the Author group in
`registry/userGroups.xml` and through the upgrade migration, but
`Repo::userGroup()->installSettings()` caps the registry's stage lists at
Production on a fresh install, so on every fresh 3.6 install (which every
e2e fleet is) no role holds stage 6 (rr3 S4: every "Done" box unchecked on
the Roles grid, Author included). The per-version gate itself passes (the
screen offers Save); the stage gate refuses the write. An upgraded install
that ran the migration should behave as intended; not verified here (the
harness never upgrades). The maintainer's question (2026-09-09) settled the
scope: A16 is a fresh-install defect, its install-side fix the
`<= WORKFLOW_STAGE_ID_PRODUCTION` cap in `UserGroup\Repository::installSettings()`
(`lib/pkp/classes/userGroup/Repository.php`), which drops the registry's
`6` on every fresh 3.6 site, not only on the fleets.
