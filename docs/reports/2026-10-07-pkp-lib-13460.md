## Summary

The PRs add "Immediately, when an item is created" to "Automatic DOI
Assignment" and open the DOIs page to every submitted submission that
is not declined. Three things go wrong.

**Finding 1.** With "Immediately", an editor who declines a submission
whose "Published Manuscript Under Review" version is already published
deletes that published version's DOI and its galleys' DOIs. The public
article page stops showing the DOI, silently, and nothing on screen
brings it back. This is the open peer review workflow the option was
made for.

**Finding 2.** With "Immediately", "Revert Decline" does not assign
again the DOIs the decline deleted, although the change means it to.
The reactivated submission has no DOI until someone presses "Assign
DOIs" or the version is published.

**Finding 3.** The DOIs page no longer lists a declined submission that
carries a DOI, under any setting, the default "Upon reaching the
copyediting stage" included. Before the change the page listed it, and
the page is the only screen where that DOI can be edited, marked or
deposited. The new filter "In Copyediting, Production or Published"
also leaves out a submission back at Review that carries a DOI, which
the previous list showed.

## Finding 1 — Declining a submission deletes the DOI of its published "Published Manuscript Under Review" version

- **Severity** high
- **Effort** medium
- **Kind** regression
- **Affects**
  - main: OJS; OMP (code: its versions take the same stages, and the same loop reads every version's DOIs); OPS none (code: a posted preprint counts as published, so no decline is offered)

### Impact

- **Lost** The published version's DOI, its galleys' DOIs and, by the
  code, the DOIs of reviews already made public. The article page drops
  the DOI and nobody is told. A DOI readers may already cite no longer
  appears on the page it points to.
- **Who** Journals on "Immediately" that publish versions under review
  (the open peer review workflow the option was made for) and then
  decline the submission. Only DOIs still "Unregistered" are deleted:
  journals that deposit by hand, or register DOIs outside OJS, and any
  DOI not yet deposited.
- **Way round** None on screen. The declined submission is no longer on
  the DOIs page (Finding 3), and a new DOI would not be the one
  already published.

High: a published page silently loses its DOI, with no way to restore
it, in a setup that is not the default. Automatic deposit at
publication spares it ("Submitted" or "Registered" DOIs are kept).

### Steps to reproduce

Preconditions: PKP's default test dataset for `main` (OJS), the PRs
checked out (ojs `2f8c53b706`, lib/pkp `246e5387f6`).

1. Sign in as `dbarnes` (password `dbarnesdbarnes`).
2. Open "Settings" › "Distribution" › "DOIs" › "Setup". Set "DOI
   Prefix" to `10.1234`, "Automatic DOI Assignment" to "Immediately,
   when an item is created (only with the default DOI suffix)" and "DOI
   Format" to "Default". Press "Save".
3. Open submission 7, "Developing efficacy beliefs in the classroom"
   (Review, round 1).
4. In "Publication", press "Schedule For Publication". In "Review
   Publishing Details" choose "Published Manuscript Under Review" and
   "Major Revision", choose "Assign To Current/Back Issue" with "Vol. 1
   No. 2 (2014)", and leave "Associated review round" as it arrives.
   Press "Confirm", then "Publish".
5. Open the article's public page
   (`/index.php/publicknowledge/article/view/7`). It shows the DOI
   `https://doi.org/10.1234/…`.
6. Back in the workflow, the Review stage still offers "Decline
   Submission". Press it and "Record Decision".
7. Reload the public page.

**Expected.** The published version keeps the DOI it was published
with: `https://doi.org/10.1234/…` on its page. Before the change no
decline touched a DOI.

**Observed.** The page, still listing "Versions" "2026-10-07 (Published
Manuscript Under Review 1.0)", shows no DOI. The publication and its
galley carry no DOI any more, and the journal holds no DOI rows.

### Cause

`AssignDOIs::handleDecline()` (lib/pkp
`classes/observers/listeners/AssignDOIs.php:124-131`) runs on every
decline under "Immediately". It loops over
`Repo::doi()->getDoisForSubmission($submissionId)` and deletes each DOI
whose status is `Doi::STATUS_UNREGISTERED`. The issue's rule is that a
declined submission should not keep DOIs for items that were never out.
But `getDoisForSubmission()` (ojs `classes/doi/Repository.php:188`)
returns the DOIs of every version, published or not: the publication's,
its galleys' and the completed reviews'. Deleting a `dois` row empties
`publications.doi_id` and the galley's `doi_id` (the foreign key is
`nullOnDelete`), so the published version loses its DOI.

A published "Published Manuscript Under Review" or "Author Original"
version leaves the submission "Queued" (only a published Version of
Record makes it "Published",
`PKP\submission\Repository::getStatusByPublications()`), so "Decline
Submission" stays on offer.

Reach:

- Galley DOIs of the published version: deleted (walked).
- Review DOIs of reviews already made public: deleted (code,
  `getDoisForSubmission()` reads `getCompletedReviewAssignments()`).
- OMP: the chapter, publication format and proof file DOIs of a
  published version (code, omp `classes/doi/Repository.php`).
- A DOI row shared by an unpublished minor version and a published one
  (`getMinorVersionsWithSameDoi()`) goes with the unpublished one
  (code).

### Proposed fix

In `handleDecline()`, delete only DOIs that nothing published uses. Loop
over the submission's publications, skip the published ones, and take
the DOIs of the others with `Repo::doi()->getDoisForPublication()`,
which each app already implements per kind. Then keep any of those that
a published publication also uses (a minor version shares its source's
row). OJS's `getDoisForPublication()` includes the version's review
DOIs, so a published version's public reviews keep theirs too.

```php
$publications = Repo::submission()->get($submissionId)->getData('publications');
$keep = $publications->filter(fn ($p) => $p->getData('status') === Publication::STATUS_PUBLISHED)
    ->flatMap(fn ($p) => Repo::doi()->getDoisForPublication($p))->all();
foreach ($publications->filter(fn ($p) => $p->getData('status') !== Publication::STATUS_PUBLISHED) as $publication) {
    foreach (array_diff(Repo::doi()->getDoisForPublication($publication), $keep) as $doiId) {
        $doi = Repo::doi()->get($doiId);
        if ($doi?->getStatus() === Doi::STATUS_UNREGISTERED) {
            Repo::doi()->delete($doi);
        }
    }
}
```

**Alternatives**

- Leave every DOI of a submission that has a published version. This is
  simpler, but an unpublished later version keeps DOIs for items that
  were never out.
- Delete nothing on decline, as under "Upon reaching the copyediting
  stage". This drops what the developer's comment promises ("its
  unregistered DOIs are deleted").

**What goes with it**

- A unit test that a decline keeps a published version's DOIs and
  deletes an unpublished version's.
- No stored data to repair (unmerged).

Not tried: the checkouts stay at the PR heads for the rest of the
review.

Medium: one listener in pkp-lib, but the shared-row and review-DOI cases
need care and a test.

## Finding 2 — "Revert Decline" does not assign again the DOIs the decline deleted

- **Severity** low
- **Effort** small
- **Kind** intention gap
- **Affects**
  - main: OJS, OMP, OPS

### Impact

- **Lost** Nothing for good. The reactivated submission has no DOI for
  its publication or galleys, although "Immediately" promises one from
  creation.
- **Who** Editors on "Immediately" who revert a decline. The developer's
  comment says: "If the decline is reverted, the DOIs are assigned
  again."
- **Way round** "DOIs" › "Bulk Actions" › "Assign DOIs" on the
  submission, which the page lists under "Needs DOI". Publishing the
  version also assigns them.

Low: nothing is lost, and the DOIs come back with "Assign DOIs" or at
publication. Something that relies on the DOI before publication would
raise it.

### Steps to reproduce

Preconditions: PKP's default test dataset for `main` (OJS), the PRs
checked out.

1. Sign in as `dbarnes` and set the DOIs "Setup" as in Finding 1 step 2.
2. Open "DOIs" › "Articles". Tick submission 4, "Computer Skill
   Requirements for New and Existing Teachers: Implications for Policy
   and Practice". Choose "Bulk Actions" › "Assign DOIs", then "Assign
   DOIs" in the window: "Items successfully assigned new DOIs". (The
   submission predates the setting. A submission made after it gets its
   DOI at submission.)
3. Open submission 4 (Submission stage), press "Decline Submission"
   and "Record Decision". The DOI is deleted, as the change intends.
4. Press "Revert Decline" and "Record Decision". The window reads
   "Submission Reactivated" and "The submission, …, is now active in
   the submission stage."
5. Open "DOIs" › "Articles" and press the filter "Needs DOI".

**Expected.** Submission 4 has a DOI again, so it is not listed under
"Needs DOI".

**Observed.** Submission 4 is listed under "Needs DOI". Its publication
carries no DOI. The same happens with "Revert Decline" on a press
(Submission stage) and on a preprint server (Production stage).

### Cause

The revert branch of `AssignDOIs::handleDecline()` (lib/pkp
`classes/observers/listeners/AssignDOIs.php:132`) runs only when
`$event->submission->getData('status') === Submission::STATUS_DECLINED`.
It relies on the comment at line 122: "The event's submission still has
the status from before the decision". That assumption is wrong.

`PKP\decision\Repository::add()` (`classes/decision/Repository.php:271`)
calls `$decisionType->runAdditionalActions()` before it fires
`DecisionAdded` (line 274). `DecisionType::runAdditionalActions()`
(`classes/decision/DecisionType.php:203-204`) calls
`Repo::submission()->updateStatus($submission, $this->getNewStatus())`,
and that sets the new status on the same object
(`classes/submission/Repository.php:741`). By the time the listener
reads the status, it is already "Queued", so `createDois()` at line 134
never runs.

Reach: every revert, in every app. That covers
`RevertInitialDecline` (Submission stage), `RevertDecline` (Review) and
OPS's `RevertDecline` (Production), each walked.

### Proposed fix

Recognise the revert by its decision type, not by a status that has
already changed:

```php
} elseif ($event->decisionType instanceof \PKP\decision\types\RevertDecline
    || $event->decisionType instanceof \PKP\decision\types\RevertInitialDecline) {
    Repo::submission()->createDois(Repo::submission()->get($submissionId));
}
```

OPS's `APP\decision\types\RevertDecline` extends
`RevertInitialDecline`, so this covers all three apps. Drop the
misleading comment.

**Alternatives**

- Carry the previous status in `DecisionAdded`. This changes an event
  other listeners and plugins receive.
- Treat any decision whose new status is "Queued" as a revert. That
  also fires on "Return to Workflow", which is harmless (`createDois()`
  fills only missing DOIs) but wider than intended.

**What goes with it** A unit test: a decline followed by its revert
leaves the publication with a DOI.

Not tried: the checkouts stay at the PR heads for the rest of the
review.

Small: one condition in one listener, and a test.

## Finding 3 — The DOIs page no longer lists a declined submission that carries a DOI

- **Severity** medium
- **Effort** medium
- **Kind** regression
- **Affects**
  - main: OJS, OMP

### Impact

- **Lost** Access to the DOI. A declined submission that carries a DOI
  is on no screen where the DOI can be edited, marked registered or
  unregistered, or deposited. Under "Upon reaching the copyediting
  stage" (the default) the DOI stays stored and, if published, public.
- **Who** Journal and press managers whose submission got a DOI and was
  declined later. That happens under the default setting (accepted,
  sent back to review, declined), and under every setting for a
  declined submission with a published version. Under "Immediately" it
  also covers DOIs already "Submitted" or "Registered", which the
  decline keeps.
- **Way round** "Revert Decline" puts the submission back on the page.
  Decline it again after the DOI work.

Medium: the DOI cannot be managed while the submission is declined,
silently, but there is a clumsy way round on screen, and the state is
uncommon.

The issue asks to "Make all submissions (excluding declined ones) show
up in the DOI management page". Leaving out declined submissions is
right for the submissions the page newly lists, which carry no DOI. The
regression is that declined submissions which carry a DOI, and which
the page listed before, are dropped as well.

### Steps to reproduce

Preconditions: PKP's default test dataset for `main` (OJS), the PRs
checked out.

1. Sign in as `dbarnes`. In "Settings" › "Distribution" › "DOIs" ›
   "Setup", set "DOI Prefix" to `10.1234`. Leave "Automatic DOI
   Assignment" at "Upon reaching the copyediting stage". Press "Save".
2. Open submission 7, "Developing efficacy beliefs in the classroom"
   (Review), press "Accept Submission" and record the decision. The
   submission is in Copyediting and gets its DOI.
3. Press "Move to Review" and "Record Decision".
4. Open "DOIs" › "Articles". Submission 7 is listed with its DOI. Press
   the filter "In Copyediting, Production or Published".
5. Back in submission 7, press "Decline Submission" and "Record
   Decision".
6. Open "DOIs" › "Articles" and search for "efficacy".

**Expected.** Step 4's filter keeps submission 7, as the previous list
did ("shows the previous list", the developer's comment). Step 6 lists
submission 7 with its DOI, as the page did before the change for every
submission carrying a DOI.

**Observed.** Step 4's filter lists no submission 7. Step 6 lists
nothing. The DOI is still stored, "Unregistered". The same happens on
a press with a book (`GET /api/v1/submissions?onDoiPage=1` leaves the
declined book out).

### Cause

The DOIs page's list is `addOnDoiPageFilterToQuery()` in each app's
Collector. At the PR head (ojs `classes/submission/Collector.php:184-200`,
omp `classes/submission/Collector.php:137-153`) it reads:

```php
$q->where('s.submission_progress', '')
    ->where('s.status', '!=', Submission::STATUS_DECLINED)
    ->when($this->inEditingOrPublished, /* stage 4 or 5, or a published publication */);
```

Before the change (ojs `1b0f84edae` lines 186-275, omp `866d8d3dd3`
lines 139-160), the page listed a submission in Copyediting or
Production, or any submission with a publication that was published or
carried a DOI. For a journal that means a publication, galley or review
DOI; for a press, a publication, chapter or format DOI
(`orWhereNotNull('pOnDoiPage.doi_id')` and its siblings).

The PRs dropped that DOI branch twice:

- The new status condition removes declined submissions whatever they
  carry.
- The new filter's subquery keeps only `pOnDoiPage.status =
  PUBLISHED`, so a submission back at Review, or at Submission with a
  DOI, falls out of the "previous list".

Reach: OJS and OMP, walked. OPS lists by its own query, which the PRs
leave alone.

### Proposed fix

Keep the base's DOI subquery and use it in both places. Move it into a
method of each Collector, say `whereCarriesDoi(Builder $q)`, holding
the base's joins and `orWhereNotNull` branches for the enabled kinds.
Then:

```php
$q->where('s.submission_progress', '')
    ->where(fn (Builder $q) => $q
        ->where('s.status', '!=', Submission::STATUS_DECLINED)
        ->orWhereIn('s.submission_id', fn (Builder $q) => $this->whereCarriesDoi($q)))
    ->when($this->inEditingOrPublished, fn (Builder $q) => $q->where(fn (Builder $q) => $q
        ->whereIn('s.stage_id', [WORKFLOW_STAGE_ID_EDITING, WORKFLOW_STAGE_ID_PRODUCTION])
        ->orWhereIn('s.submission_id', fn (Builder $q) => $this->whereCarriesDoi($q)))); // published or carrying a DOI
```

**Alternatives**

- A "Declined" filter on the page. This needs a UI change and keeps the
  DOIs out of the default list.
- Exclude declined submissions only when they carry no DOI, written
  inline twice. This is the same query without the shared method.

**What goes with it**

- An e2e scenario in U45: a declined submission with a DOI stays on the
  page, and the filter keeps a submission back at Review with a DOI.
- The OJS and OMP Collector tests.

Not tried: the checkouts stay at the PR heads for the rest of the
review.

Medium: two app repos (OJS and OMP), each with its query and test.
