## Summary

A journal deposits its review DOIs with Crossref. An editor accepts a
work without confirming its public review (no "Mark as Complete", and
the decision's "Notify Reviewers" email skipped), so the review already
has a DOI but no public page. The work is published, and "Deposit All"
runs. The review's DOI turns "Submitted", but the deposit leaves the
review out, because the review is not yet confirmed.

When the editor confirms the review later, the DOIs page shows it
"Submitted", and "Deposit All" never picks it up again. The review's DOI
is never registered, and nothing says so. A deposit of that one work
from the DOIs page sends it.

## Impact

- **Lost** The review's DOI registration. The page says the DOI was
  submitted; no deposit carried it, and the public peer-review API
  gives its doi.org link, which does not resolve. Nobody is told.
- **Who** Journals with "Peer Review" ticked under "Items with DOIs"
  (off by default), Crossref as the agency and reviews shown publicly,
  whose editor confirms a review only after the first deposit of its
  published work, by "Deposit All" or the automatic deposit.
- **Way round** "Deposit DOIs" on the work once the review is
  confirmed sends it (code). Nothing on the page prompts it.

Medium: a DOI the journal shows is silently never registered, for the
few reviews confirmed after their work's first deposit, and a per-work
deposit sets it right. It is not low, since a deposit is lost, not just
a status.

## Cause

Round 4 adds a condition to
`reviewAssignment\DAO::getExportableDOIsPeerReviewIds()` (lib/pkp
`classes/submission/reviewAssignment/DAO.php:204`): a review is exported
and deposited only once an editor has confirmed it (`date_considered` or
`date_acknowledged`), as the public peer-review record shows only
confirmed reviews. The `DepositSubmission` job reads that list to
dispatch `DepositPeerReview` (lib/pkp `jobs/doi/DepositSubmission.php:74-79`),
and `DepositPeerReview` checks it again.

The status side did not follow. "Deposit All" (lib/pkp
`classes/doi/Repository.php:292`) marks "Submitted" every DOI of OJS's
`doi\DAO::getAllDepositableSubmissionIds()` (ojs `classes/doi/DAO.php:37`),
whose peer-review branch (`:71-82`) asks for a DOI, public visibility and
a published linked version, but no confirmation. So it marks a DOI the
deposit no longer sends. Nothing resets it: Crossref's answer to the
work's deposit updates `getPublishedDoisForSubmission()` (crossref
`CrossrefExportPlugin.php:423`), whose reviews come from
`getCompletedReviewAssignments()`, which leaves out every review of a
published work (lib/pkp `classes/submission/reviewAssignment/Collector.php:486-489`,
`pkp/pkp-lib#13450`). "Deposit All" then skips the "Submitted" DOI
(ojs `classes/doi/DAO.php:117`).

At the tips and in round 3 the deposit list had no confirmation
condition, so the same "Deposit All" sent the review with its work, and
Crossref's answer set its own status (`updateDepositStatus()` for the
review). The rule round 4 adds is right; the selection that marks
"Submitted" was left on the old one.

When a review has a DOI before confirmation: under "Upon reaching the
copyediting stage" (the default), the move to Copyediting gives every
completed public review of the version a DOI, confirmed or not (ojs
`classes/publication/Repository.php:371-386`; U45 Rule 7). Under
"Immediately…" a review gets its DOI when it is confirmed
(`reviewAssignment\Repository::edit()`, lib/pkp `:224-226`), but "Assign
DOIs" on the DOIs page and the publication of a "Published Manuscript
Under Review" version still give one to an unconfirmed review, by the
same `createDois()` (code).

Reach:

- "Deposit All" on the DOIs page and the automatic deposit, which runs
  the same method (`jobs/doi/DepositContext.php:65`) (walked, through
  "Deposit All").
- "Deposit DOIs" on a work whose current version is a published
  "Published Manuscript Under Review": the work is not "published" for
  `getCompletedReviewAssignments()`, so the unconfirmed review's DOI is
  marked "Submitted", and Crossref's success for the work marks it
  "Registered" without having sent it (code, not driven).
- A neighbour, older than round 4 (walked): "Deposit All" returns a
  review's DOI with no work id (the selection joins the work through the
  publication's own DOI, ojs `classes/doi/DAO.php:40-43`), so it queues a
  deposit only when the work's article DOI is outstanding too. With the
  article already "Registered", a confirmed review's DOI is marked
  "Submitted" and no deposit is queued. Any fix that holds the review
  back until confirmation leads into this.
- "Export DOIs" reads the round-4 list and leaves the unconfirmed review
  out, as meant (code).

## Proposed fix

A proposal; the team decides. Let the selection that marks "Submitted"
follow the deposit's rule, and let it queue the work for a review that
is outstanding on its own. In ojs `classes/doi/DAO.php`
`getAllDepositableSubmissionIds()`:

```diff
         $q = DB::table($this->table, 'd')
             ->leftJoin('publications as p', 'd.doi_id', '=', 'p.doi_id')
             ->leftJoin('submissions as s', 'p.publication_id', '=', 's.current_publication_id')
+            ->leftJoin('review_assignments as dra', 'dra.doi_id', '=', 'd.doi_id')
 ...
                                 // Peer reviews should be public to be considered
-                                ->where('ra.is_review_publicly_visible', '=', 1);
+                                ->where('ra.is_review_publicly_visible', '=', 1)
+                                // Only reviews confirmed by an editor are deposited, see getExportableDOIsPeerReviewIds()
+                                ->where(fn (Builder $q) => $q->whereNotNull('ra.date_considered')->orWhereNotNull('ra.date_acknowledged'));
 ...
-        return $q->get(['s.submission_id', 'd.doi_id']);
+        return $q->get([DB::raw('COALESCE(s.submission_id, dra.submission_id) AS submission_id'), 'd.doi_id']);
```

and in lib/pkp `classes/doi/Repository.php` `depositAll()`, dispatch
one `DepositSubmission` per work (`array_unique()` on the ids), since a
work's article and review rows now both carry its id.

The per-work path follows the same rule: in ojs
`classes/doi/Repository.php`, the peer-review part of
`getDoisForPublication()` (`:279-288`) and of `getDoisForSubmission()`
(`:222`) takes only confirmed reviews: a collector with
`filterByCompleted(true)` and `filterByIsConfirmedByEditor(true)` in
place of `getCompletedReviewAssignments()`. `pkp/pkp-lib#13450` rewrites
the same lookup, so the two belong together.

**Alternatives**

- Give a review no DOI before confirmation, under every timing: skip
  unconfirmed reviews in `createDois()` and assign on confirmation once
  the work has passed its automatic moment. No set would need the
  condition, but it moves U45 Rule 7's moment, needs the timing logic in
  the review repository, and still needs the work id for "Deposit All".
- Drop the confirmation condition from the deposit list again, as in
  round 3. Crossref would then get reviews whose page shows nothing,
  which round 4 set out to stop.
- Build the "Deposit All" subquery from the deposit list's own query, so
  the rule lives in one place. Cleaner, but it reshapes a public DAO
  method in pkp-lib for one caller.

**What goes with it**

- "Mark DOIs Registered", "Unregistered" and "Needs Sync" stop touching
  an unconfirmed review's DOI on a work under review; it has no page and
  no deposit, so nothing is lost.
- A work whose article is "Registered" is sent again when only its
  review is outstanding; Crossref takes that as an update.
- No stored data to repair (unmerged).
- A unit test on `getAllDepositableSubmissionIds()` for an unconfirmed
  and a confirmed review, and the kept script below as the e2e walk.

Not tried: the checkouts stay at the PR heads for the rest of the
review.

Medium: a few lines in two OJS classes and one in pkp-lib, a behaviour
change the status actions follow, and tests.
