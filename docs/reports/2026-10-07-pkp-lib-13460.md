## Summary

A journal deposits its review DOIs with Crossref and publishes a
manuscript as "Published Manuscript Under Review" while it is still in
review. The publication gives the manuscript and its completed public
review their DOIs, before any editor has confirmed the review. When the
editor presses "Deposit DOIs" for that work on the DOIs page, the
review's DOI turns "Submitted", but the deposit leaves the review out,
because it is not yet confirmed.

Once the editor confirms the review, the DOIs page shows it "Submitted",
and "Deposit All" never picks it up. The review's DOI is never
registered, and nothing says so. A second "Deposit DOIs" on the work
sends it, but nothing prompts one.

This is the open-review workflow peer-review DOIs serve: a manuscript
published under review, with a public review the editor confirms after
the first deposit. A work published as the version of record is not
affected.

## Impact

- **Lost** The review's DOI registration. The page says the DOI was
  submitted, no deposit carried it, and its doi.org link does not
  resolve. Nobody is told.
- **Who** Journals with "Peer Review" ticked under "Items with DOIs"
  (off by default), Crossref as the agency and public reviews, whose
  editor deposits a manuscript published under review before confirming
  its review.
- **Way round** "Deposit DOIs" on the work again once the review is
  confirmed sends it (code). Nothing on the page prompts it.

Medium: a DOI the journal shows is silently never registered, for the
few reviews of manuscripts published under review that are confirmed
after their work's deposit, and a second per-work deposit sets it right.
It is not low, since a deposit is lost, not just a status.

## Steps to reproduce

Preconditions: PKP's default test dataset for `main` (OJS), the PRs
checked out (ojs `11377fcee3`, lib/pkp `6331e43478`, crossref
`f358a32628`). For the deposit to reach Crossref, a Crossref test
account; the review's statuses below do not depend on Crossref's
answer. Submission 10, "Condensing Water Availability Models to Focus
on Specific Water Management Systems", is in Review, round 1, with
completed reviews by `amccrae` and `agallego` that no editor has
confirmed and that are not public.

1. Sign in as `dbarnes` (password `dbarnesdbarnes`).
2. Open "Settings" › "Distribution" › "DOIs" › "Setup". Tick "Peer
   Review" under "Items with DOIs" beside "Articles". Type `10.1234` in
   "DOI Prefix", keep "Upon reaching the copyediting stage" under
   "Automatic DOI Assignment", and press "Save".
3. Open the "Registration" tab, choose "Crossref" under "Registration
   Agency", fill in "Depositor name", "Depositor email", "Username" and
   "Password", tick "Testing" and press "Save".
4. Open submission 10's workflow at Review, round 1. Open "Edit" on
   Aisla McCrae's review, tick "Public Visibility" ("Publicly Show
   Reviewer Comments") and save. Do not press "Mark as Complete".
5. Press "Schedule For Publication". In "Review Publishing Details"
   choose "Published Manuscript Under Review" under "Publication Stage"
   and "Major Revision", keep "Round 1" under "Associated review round",
   choose "Don't Assign To An Issue", press "Confirm", then "Publish".
   The work stays in Review; the article and McCrae's review get their
   DOIs.
6. Open "DOIs" › "Articles" and expand the work's row. It shows
   "Article: Unregistered" only (a review's row shows once confirmed).
7. Tick the work, choose "Deposit DOIs" under "Bulk Actions" and
   confirm.
8. Back in the workflow at Review, round 1, press "Read Review" on
   McCrae's review, then "Mark as Complete", and confirm.
9. Open "DOIs" › "Articles" and expand the work's row.
10. Press "Deposit All" and confirm "Deposit all DOIs".

**Expected.** Step 7 sends the article without the unconfirmed review
and leaves the review's DOI "Unregistered". Step 9 shows the review's
row "Unregistered", and step 10 sends it.

**Observed.** Right after step 7 the review's DOI reads "Submitted", and
the list of reviews the deposit sends is empty. Step 9:

```
Article: Submitted
Peer Review 14: Submitted
```

(The review's number is the install's own.) The deposit's list holds
the review after step 8, but step 10 takes nothing for the work and both
DOIs stay "Submitted".

Control: the same "Deposit DOIs" on a work published as the version of
record leaves its unconfirmed review's DOI "Unregistered", and the
deposit does not send it either.

## Cause

"Deposit DOIs" (`PKPDoiController::depositSubmissions()`, lib/pkp
`api/v1/dois/PKPDoiController.php:550-555`) queues a `DepositSubmission`
job per work and marks "Submitted" every DOI of
`getPublishedDoisForSubmission()` (lib/pkp
`classes/doi/Repository.php:435`). That reads OJS
`getDoisForPublication()` per published version, whose peer-review part
(ojs `classes/doi/Repository.php:279-288`) takes
`getCompletedReviewAssignments()` (lib/pkp
`classes/publication/Repository.php:1265`): completed reviews, with no
confirmation or visibility condition.

That lookup leaves out every review of a work whose status is Published
(`reviewAssignment\Collector`, lib/pkp `:486-491`,
`pkp/pkp-lib#13450`), and only a published version of record sets that
status (`getStatusByPublications()`). A work whose current version is a
published "Published Manuscript Under Review" stays Queued, so every
completed review of the version is taken, confirmed or not.

The job sends only the reviews of
`reviewAssignment\DAO::getExportableDOIsPeerReviewIds()` (lib/pkp
`classes/submission/reviewAssignment/DAO.php:196`, dispatched from
`jobs/doi/DepositSubmission.php:73-79`), which since round 4 requires an
editor's confirmation (`:203-204`) and public visibility. Round 5 brought
the "Deposit All" selection to that rule (ojs `classes/doi/DAO.php:85`);
the per-work set still follows the old one, so it marks a DOI the job
does not send.

Nothing sets it right. On Crossref's success the plugin's
`updateDepositStatus()` for the work (crossref
`CrossrefExportPlugin.php:420-423`) sets every DOI of
`getPublishedDoisForSubmission()`, the unsent review's included,
"Registered" (code; no deposit can succeed on the test installs). After
confirmation, "Deposit All" skips the DOI: it takes only "Unregistered",
"Error" and "Needs Sync" (ojs `classes/doi/DAO.php:121`).

At the tips the deposit list had no confirmation condition, so the same
"Deposit DOIs" sent the review with its work, and the review's own
deposit set its status. The rule round 4 adds is right; the per-work
lookup was left on the old one.

Reach:

- "Deposit DOIs" on a work whose current version is a published
  "Published Manuscript Under Review" (walked), and its successful
  deposit marking the unsent review "Registered" (code).
- "Mark DOIs Registered" on such a work marks an unconfirmed review's
  DOI "Registered" (walked). The same at the tips, older than the PR.
- "Mark DOIs Unregistered" and "Mark DOIs Needs Sync" read
  `getDoisForSubmission()` (ojs `classes/doi/Repository.php:224`), the
  same lookup (code).
- A review whose "Public Visibility" was unticked after it got a DOI:
  the per-work actions mark it too, as at the tips (code, not driven).
- A work published as the version of record: no review DOI is in the
  per-work set at all (`pkp/pkp-lib#13450`), so the status and the
  deposit agree (walked, the control).

## Proposed fix

A proposal; the team decides. Let the per-work set take the reviews the
deposit sends. In ojs `classes/doi/Repository.php`, in both
`getDoisForPublication()` (`:281`) and `getDoisForSubmission()`
(`:224`):

```diff
-            $reviewAssignments = Repo::publication()->getCompletedReviewAssignments([$publication->getId()]);
+            // Only the reviews a deposit sends, see getExportableDOIsPeerReviewIds()
+            $reviewAssignments = Repo::reviewAssignment()->getCollector()
+                ->filterByPublicationIds([$publication->getId()])
+                ->filterByCompleted(true)
+                ->filterByIsConfirmedByEditor(true)
+                ->filterByIsPubliclyVisible(true)
+                ->getMany();
```

These are the filters the public peer-review data already uses (lib/pkp
`classes/publication/Repository.php:1327-1333`). `filterByCompleted()`
keeps the published-status exclusion that `pkp/pkp-lib#13450` rewrites,
so the two changes belong together.

**Alternatives**

- Add the conditions to `getCompletedReviewAssignments()` in pkp-lib.
  It also feeds `createDois()` (ojs
  `classes/publication/Repository.php:374`), so a review confirmed after
  its work reached Copyediting would get no DOI under the default
  timing.
- Mark "Submitted" from the job, after it has worked out what it sends,
  rather than in the controller. Right in principle, but it changes the
  job's contract for every registration agency.
- Drop the confirmation condition from the deposit list again, as at
  the tips. Crossref would then get reviews whose page shows nothing,
  which round 4 set out to stop.

**What goes with it**

- "Mark DOIs Registered", "Unregistered" and "Needs Sync" stop touching
  an unconfirmed or non-public review's DOI on a manuscript published
  under review. It has no public page and no deposit, so nothing is
  lost.
- Crossref's success marks only what the deposit sent.
- No stored data to repair: peer-review DOIs are on `main` only, and the
  change is unmerged.
- A unit test on `getDoisForPublication()` for a confirmed and an
  unconfirmed public review, and the kept script below as the e2e walk.

Not tried: the checkouts stay at the PR heads for the rest of the
review.

Small: the same few lines in two methods of one OJS class, following a
filter pattern pkp-lib already uses, and a unit test.
