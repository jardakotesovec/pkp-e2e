## Summary

A journal deposits peer-review DOIs with Crossref. A published
article's review has a registered DOI, and an editor then unticks the
review's "Public Visibility". The DOI stays, as intended, and the review
leaves the public page and the DOIs page. When a manager next presses
"Deposit DOIs" for the article, the hidden review's DOI turns
"Submitted", although the deposit leaves the review out.

No page shows the change until the review is made public again: its row
then reads "Submitted" for a deposit that never carried it. The DOI was
registered before it was hidden, so it still resolves; only its status
is wrong.

It needs a review taken out of public view after its DOI was sent or
marked "Registered", and a later deposit of its article.

## Impact

- **Lost** A correct status on the hidden review's DOI. Nobody is told.
- **Who** Journals with "Peer Review" ticked under "Items with DOIs"
  (off by default) and a registration agency, whose editor hides a
  review after its DOI was registered and later deposits the article
  again.
- **Way round** For a registered DOI none is needed: Crossref's success
  marks it "Registered" again (code). "Mark DOIs Registered" on the work
  sets it by hand.

Low: a status on a rarely met state, while the DOI itself was
registered earlier. It would be medium if a review hidden while its DOI
read "Error" or "Needs Sync" were then marked "Registered" by a deposit
that never carried it, which the code says happens and no walk has
shown.

## Cause

"Deposit DOIs" (`PKPDoiController::depositSubmissions()`, lib/pkp
`api/v1/dois/PKPDoiController.php:550-555`) queues a `DepositSubmission`
job per work and marks "Submitted" every DOI of
`getPublishedDoisForSubmission()` (lib/pkp
`classes/doi/Repository.php:435`). That reads OJS
`getDoisForPublication()` per published version, whose peer-review part
(ojs `classes/doi/Repository.php:279-288`) takes the DOI of every review
`getCompletedReviewAssignments()` returns (lib/pkp
`classes/publication/Repository.php:1265`).

Round 6 changed that lookup from `filterByCompleted(true)` to
`filterByIsConfirmedByEditor(true)`, so that reviews of a published work
get DOIs (`pkp/pkp-lib#13450`). It has no visibility condition, before
or after. Up to round 5 the published-status exclusion in
`filterByCompleted()` (`reviewAssignment\Collector`, lib/pkp
`:486-490`) left every review of a published article out of the
per-work set, so a hidden review's kept DOI kept its status. Now a
confirmed review is in the set whether it is public or not.

Every other place that decides which reviews have DOIs pairs
confirmation with public visibility: the job's list
`getExportableDOIsPeerReviewIds()` (lib/pkp
`classes/submission/reviewAssignment/DAO.php:196`, `:206`), the DOIs
page row `getReviewDoiItemsGroupedByPublication()` (lib/pkp
`classes/publication/Repository.php:1330-1332`), the round's own
`canHaveDoi()` (lib/pkp `classes/submission/reviewAssignment/Repository.php:249`),
and OJS `createDois()`, which skips a hidden review itself (ojs
`classes/publication/Repository.php:376`). The per-work set is the one
that does not, so it marks a DOI the job does not send.

Crossref's answer follows the same set: the plugin's
`updateDepositStatus()` for the work (crossref
`CrossrefExportPlugin.php:420-423`) sets every DOI of
`getPublishedDoisForSubmission()`, the hidden review's included,
"Registered" on success and "Error" on failure (code; no deposit can
succeed on the test installs).

Reach:

- "Deposit DOIs" on a published work with a hidden review that kept its
  DOI (walked).
- Crossref's success marking that DOI "Registered" with nothing sent;
  for a DOI hidden while "Error" or "Needs Sync", a status it never
  earned (code).
- "Mark DOIs Registered" (`:602`, the same set) marks it "Registered"
  (code).
- "Mark DOIs Unregistered" and "Mark DOIs Needs Sync" (`:655`, `:708`)
  read OJS `getDoisForSubmission()` (`:224`), the same lookup: they reset
  the hidden review's DOI, and an "Unregistered" DOI then stays on a
  review nobody sees, the state `removeUnregisteredDoi()` exists to
  avoid, since only a confirmation or visibility change removes it
  (code).
- Publishing or unpublishing a version marks the set "Needs Sync"
  (`markStale`, lib/pkp `classes/publication/Repository.php:704-842`),
  the hidden review's DOI included, and "Deposit All" (public reviews
  only) never clears it (code, not driven).
- A confirmed public review: the per-work set, the job's list and the
  DOIs page agree (walked, the control).
- OMP and OPS have no peer-review DOIs (code).

## Proposed fix

A proposal; the team decides. Let the shared lookup take only the
reviews that can carry a DOI, as the deposit list and the DOIs page
already do. In lib/pkp `classes/publication/Repository.php`:

```diff
     public function getCompletedReviewAssignments(array $publicationIds): Enumerable
     {
         return Repo::reviewAssignment()
             ->getCollector()
             ->filterByPublicationIds($publicationIds)
             ->filterByIsConfirmedByEditor(true)
+            ->filterByIsPubliclyVisible(true)
             ->getMany();
     }
```

Its only callers are OJS's three DOI lookups (`getDoisForPublication()`,
`getDoisForSubmission()`, `createDois()`), so one line covers every
per-work action, Crossref's status update and `markStale`. The pair of
filters is the one `getReviewDoiItemsGroupedByPublication()` uses, and
`canHaveDoi()` states the same rule. The check in `createDois()` (ojs
`:376`) becomes redundant and can go. The method's docblock should then
say "confirmed by an editor and publicly visible", since the name says
less than it does.

**Alternatives**

- The same condition in OJS `getDoisForPublication()` and
  `getDoisForSubmission()`, as `createDois()` does it: the same effect,
  but the rule stays split over three OJS methods, and the next caller
  of the pkp-lib method meets the same gap.
- Mark "Submitted" from the job, once it knows what it sends, rather
  than in the controller: right in principle, but it changes the job's
  contract for every registration agency.
- Go back to `filterByCompleted(true)`: undoes `pkp/pkp-lib#13450`.

**What goes with it**

- A hidden review's DOI keeps whatever status it had through every
  per-work action and Crossref's answer, and comes back with it when the
  review is shown again. No public page changes.
- No stored data to repair: peer-review DOIs are on `main` only, and the
  change is unmerged.
- A unit test on `getDoisForPublication()` for a confirmed public and a
  confirmed hidden review, and the kept script below as the e2e walk.

Not tried: the checkouts stay at the PR heads for the rest of the
review.

Small: one line in the shared pkp-lib lookup, following the filter pair
the same class already uses, and a unit test.

## Open question (not a finding)

The removal is the team's stated intention. What it leaves open is the
way back: when an editor presses "Revert Decision" on a review's
"Complete" row, or unticks "Public Visibility", on a published article,
and then confirms the review or ticks the box again, which DOI should
the review show?

- Under "Immediately", a new one at once: on the walk the public review
  record showed `10.1234/tg14d743`, then nothing after the revert, then
  `10.1234/9y2zb432` after "Mark as Complete".
- Under "Upon reaching the copyediting stage" (and the other settings),
  none until a manager presses "Assign DOIs": the review is listed
  publicly without a DOI, and its DOIs page row reads "Needs DOI".
- A "Registered" DOI is kept and comes back unchanged.

Two readings:

1. **Keep the DOI row while the review is not public.** No list, page
   or deposit takes it then, since each requires a confirmed public
   review, so the review comes back with the DOI readers have already
   seen, under every setting. An "Unregistered" DOI is not always
   unregistered: a journal without an agency registers by hand and never
   changes the status.
2. **Mint anew, as built.** An "Unregistered" DOI was never sent to an
   agency, so nothing outside depends on it, and a new DOI at the
   setting's own moment matches what "Revert Decline" does.

Spec U45 carries it as OJS7, leaning to the second reading.
